**Free, offline voice cleanup for your recordings.** This is the first public alpha of KreoKit Voice: drop in a podcast, voice-over, interview or talking-head video, remove the background noise, polish the sound to the right loudness, compare before and after, and export one file or a whole batch. Everything runs on your own computer. No account, no upload, no watermark.

> ⚠️ **Alpha.** Expect rough edges. Please report problems at https://github.com/kreokit/voice/issues (or ⋯ → Report a problem in the app).

## What it does

- **Remove background noise:** fans, air conditioning, hum, traffic rumble, keyboard clicks. **Fast** (RNNoise, built in) cleans many times faster than real time; **Best** (DPDFNet, a one-time 10.5 MB download) is clearer and also takes some room echo away. **Strength** keeps a little of the room for a natural sound.
- **Polish:** remove low rumble, soften harsh S sounds, even out the level, and set the loudness: **Social video −14 LUFS** (TikTok, Reels, Shorts, YouTube), **Podcast −16 LUFS**, your own, or keep the original level. Peaks always stay under −1 dBTP.
- **Compare:** switch between Original and Cleaned at the same moment (key B), loudness-matched, so "louder" doesn't fool your ear. The loudness of the result is shown right away (≈) and then measured exactly.
- **Export:** WAV (24-bit), FLAC (24-bit), M4A (AAC 256 kbps) or MP3 (320 kbps). Every file is measured after writing (loudness within ±0.5 LU of the target, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP) before it's saved.
- **Videos:** the picture is copied untouched (bit for bit) and only the sound is replaced, in the same container (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM); subtitles, chapters and other audio tracks are kept. Or save just the cleaned sound as WAV, FLAC, M4A or MP3.
- **Batch:** open several files, choose the settings once, Export all: each file's loudness is measured on its own, with time left per file and in total, a free-space check, and nothing overwritten without asking (Replace or Keep both).

**Requirements:** macOS 12 Monterey or later, Windows 10 (version 1903 or later) or 11, or Linux with glibc 2.35+ (e.g. Ubuntu 22.04+). Fast works on any 64-bit computer. Best needs SSE4.2 and POPCNT on Intel / AMD (most Intel CPUs since 2009, AMD since 2011); the app tells you if yours doesn't, and Fast still works.

**AI model:** Best downloads its model (10.5 MB) the first time you use it, from this project's `models-v1` release, and checks it. That one-time download is the app's only internet use.

## Installing an unsigned app

The alpha isn't signed with paid developer certificates, so your system will warn you the first time.

**macOS**
1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **KreoKit Voice** to Applications.
2. Open it. If macOS says it "can't be opened" or "can't verify the developer", open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** (on macOS 12: **System Preferences → Security & Privacy → General**), and click **Open Anyway**, then confirm.
3. If macOS instead says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", that is the same missing signature, not real damage. Run this once in Terminal, then open the app again:
   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/KreoKit Voice.app"
   ```

**Windows**
1. Run the `…-setup.exe` installer.
2. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Linux**
- AppImage: `chmod +x KreoKit.Voice_*.AppImage`, then run it.
- Debian / Ubuntu: `sudo apt install ./KreoKit.Voice_*_amd64.deb`.

## Known limitations

- No dedicated room-echo removal yet (Best takes some echo away). Other voices in the background (a crowd, a TV) are beyond Fast; Best handles them better.
- Everything runs on the CPU. Best is about 2–3× faster than real time on an older Intel Mac; Fast much more. The computer is kept awake while exporting (macOS and Windows).
- No loop region or multi-track (one file per speaker) yet.
- The Windows and Linux downloads are large because they include a complete FFmpeg build.
